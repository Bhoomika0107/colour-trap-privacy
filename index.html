Privacy Policy

Colour Trap

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Developer: Bhoomika Patel

In short: Colour Trap does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet. The few game settings it remembers stay on your device.

About this policy

This policy explains how the Colour Trap mobile game ("the app"), developed by Bhoomika Patel ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the app on Android and iOS.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it has no sign-in or user accounts.

Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information only on your device:

  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration, colour-blind shapes, high contrast, reduce motion, memory assist and text size.
  • The last game you set up (number of players and difficulty), so it is ready next time.
  • Whether you have already seen the welcome screens.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Internet, advertising and analytics

  • The app works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.
  • There are no advertisements.
  • There are no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • No information is shared with or sold to third parties.

Permissions

On Android the app asks for the Vibrate permission only, so your phone can buzz when a guess is wrong. You can turn vibration off at any time in the app's Settings (Haptics). The app uses no other permissions.

Children's privacy

Colour Trap is a family-friendly game suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children, including children under 13.

App stores

When you download the app, Google Play or the Apple App Store may collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not control or receive that information. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Please check it from time to time.
